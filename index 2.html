What we do

One crew, three jobs

Most companies sell you lights. We handle the whole season — from the first measurement to the box going back in our warehouse, not yours.

Install

Rooflines, trees & walkways

We measure your home, run the strands with gutter-safe clips, and hide every wire. Most homes are done in under four hours, start to finish.

Supply

Commercial-grade lights & timers

C9s, mini lights, and warm-white icicles — all owned by us, sized to your house, and swapped immediately if a strand fails.

Removal

Takedown & storage

We schedule your takedown for January, coil everything by house, and store it in our warehouse until next year if you'd rather not.

How it works

Four steps, one season

  1. 1

    Walkthrough & design

    We come by to measure your roofline, trees, and walkways, then send over a layout and price before anything is scheduled.

  2. 2

    Installation

    Your crew installs on the agreed date, usually within a single afternoon. You don't need to be home.

  3. 3

    Season support

    If a strand goes dark or a timer resets, call us. Repairs through New Year's are included at no extra charge.

  4. 4

    Takedown

    We schedule removal in early January and take everything with us — coiled, labeled, and out of your way.

Questions

Before you book

Do I need to be home for the install? +

No. Once your layout is approved, our crew can install while you're at work or out of the house. We photograph the finished result and send it to you.

What happens if a bulb or strand fails mid-season? +

Call or text us and we'll come repair or replace the section, usually within a day or two, at no extra cost through New Year's.

Do you store the lights after takedown? +

Yes, if you'd like. We coil and label everything by house and keep it in our warehouse until it's time to reinstall next year.

What areas do you service? +

We currently install across the greater metro area and surrounding suburbs. See the full list of neighborhoods below, or ask us directly.

How far in advance should I book? +

Most customers book in October or early November. We can often fit in later requests, but prime December dates fill up first.
